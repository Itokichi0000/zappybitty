# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

First release of the open-source, offline edition.

- One-tap flying through candlestick charts, across 14 zones from Shibuya to the edge of the galaxy
- Items, enemies, missions, and a shop of skins, hats and trails bought with bitty earned in play
- One continue per run for 21 bitty
- Everything stays on the device: no account, no server, no ads, no tracking
- Japanese and English
